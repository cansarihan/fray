About Fray

Fray tests one of your own network services against a model of its protocol. The model lists the conversations the service supports and the fields in each message; Fray walks them once to be sure they work, then sends every message again with one part replaced by something hostile — an empty value, a megabyte of text, a boundary number, a broken line ending.

It reports where the service hangs, drops the connection or stops speaking its own protocol, and it keeps the exact sequence that did it, so each finding can be replayed line by line.

The target defaults to loopback. Point Fray at another host only with -allow-remote, and only at one you are authorized to test.
